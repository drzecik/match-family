# Source policy

Evidence priority is: official manual, official compatibility list, official product specification, official adapter documentation, official firmware documentation, then a reputable independent test as supporting context.

Each evidence record stores source type, canonical URL, retrieval or publication date, exact claim scope, notes, reviewer identity, and verification status. A source proving one dimension cannot be generalized to another. Screenshots, reseller copy, search snippets, and connector appearance are insufficient for safety claims.

No scraping is part of the product. Imports use manually supplied files, licensed feeds, or official downloadable material. Imported content is data, never executable code. Stale reports flag facts for review but do not silently invalidate historical evidence.

Synthetic demo evidence uses `synthetic://` URLs and is always non-production.
