# Recommendation policy

Ranking order is fixed: safety and verified compatibility, user requirements, reuse of owned gear, budget, quality and function, availability, price, then affiliate attributes. Candidate, unknown, incompatible, unsafe, and deprecated paths are excluded from recommendations.

Sponsored products must be labelled, may not hide cheaper eligible choices, and receive no compatibility boost. The ranking API accepts compatibility as an immutable input; commercial data can only break ties after all product-value criteria.

Every result exposes exclusions, limitations, missing facts, required intermediates, and price freshness. “Best” pages are not generated automatically.
